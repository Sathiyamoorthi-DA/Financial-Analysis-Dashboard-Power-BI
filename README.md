# Financial Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive financial analysis dashboard designed to help executive management monitor revenue, expenses, gross and net profitability, cash flow, budget variance, product margins, and business unit performance to drive business performance and improve strategic decision-making.

**Created by:** Sathiyamoorthi S  
**Tool:** Microsoft Power BI  
**Reporting Period:** January 2024 – December 2025  
**Domain:** Financial Analytics | MIS Reporting

## Dashboard Preview

[![Financial Analysis Dashboard](Screenshots/financial-analysis-dashboard.png)](https://github.com/Sathiyamoorthi-DA/Financial-analysis-power-BI/blob/main/Screenshots/financial-analysis-dashboard.png)

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to build an executive-level financial dashboard that consolidates core financial indicators into a single actionable view. It enables stakeholders to monitor overall financial performance, evaluate profit margins, track actual vs. budget variances, and analyze performance across products and business units.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Summary KPIs
Provides top-level metrics on Revenue, Expenses, Gross Profit, Net Profit, Profit Margins, EBITDA, Cash Flow, and Budget Variance with YoY comparisons.

### 2. Revenue vs. Expenses & Profitability Trend
Tracks monthly figures from January to December to monitor revenue trajectory against operational expenses, alongside Gross Profit ($M) and Net Profit ($M) trends.

### 3. Expense Breakdown
Visualizes expense distribution across major operational categories:
- **Salaries & Benefits:** 2.82M (38.5%)
- **Operations:** 1.64M (22.4%)
- **Other:** 1.15M (15.7%)
- **Marketing:** 0.94M (12.8%)
- **Technology:** 0.78M (10.6%)

### 4. Actual vs. Budget Comparison
Compares target budget values against actual performance for Revenue (+4.2%), Expenses (+2.1%), Gross Profit (+7.3%), and Net Profit (+12.3%).

### 5. Revenue by Business Unit
Ranks core revenue channels including Products ($4.25M - 34.0%), Services ($3.09M - 24.8%), and Consulting ($2.40M - 19.2%).

### 6. Profit Margin Trend
Monitors monthly fluctuation in profit margin percentage to ensure sustained operational profitability throughout the year.

### 7. Cash Flow Analysis
Tracks monthly Cash Inflow ($M), Cash Outflow ($M), and net Cash Flow ($M) to maintain oversight of liquidity.

### 8. Top Performance Products
Ranks top revenue and profit-generating offerings:
1. **Laptop:** $2,848,275.54 Revenue | $926,309.96 Profit | 32.52% Margin
2. **Smartphone:** $2,408,652.16 Revenue | $719,821.48 Profit | 29.88% Margin
3. **Tablet:** $1,235,410.84 Revenue | $312,888.74 Profit | 25.33% Margin
4. **Accessories:** $983,571.28 Revenue | $221,566.88 Profit | 22.53% Margin
5. **Wearables:** $764,879.25 Revenue | $160,045.03 Profit | 20.92% Margin

### 9. Interactive Slicers
Allows dynamic filtering across Year, Month, Region, Business Unit, Department, Product, Customer, and Expense Category.

## Business Questions Addressed

- How are monthly revenue and expenses trending throughout the year?
- How effectively is the business converting revenue into gross and net profits?
- Which business units generate the highest revenue contribution?
- What are the largest drivers of overall operational expenses?
- How do actual financial outcomes compare to planned budget targets?
- Which individual products yield the highest profit margins?
- How healthy is the organization's overall cash flow position?

## How to Explore the Dashboard
Download the Power BI report file (.pbix) if included in the repository.

Open it using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.

Update the data source path if prompted.

Click Refresh to reload the dataset.

Use the dynamic slicers across the top bar to filter performance by year, region, business unit, and department.
